Privacy policy

Sound Sprout privacy policy

Effective [date to be set on publication]. Sound Sprout is published by Dotair Studio, the studio name of DOTAIR LLC (“we”). Sound Sprout lets you play musical instruments and save recordings of sounds created inside the app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

What we collect

Nothing. Sound Sprout does not collect, store on a server, sell, or share any personal information or usage data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking, and it makes no network connections of its own. Dotair Studio does not operate a server that receives anything from the app.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your note and rhythm patterns, recordings, favorites, last selected instrument, and a few milestones used to decide when to ask for an App Store rating. All of this stays in the app’s storage on your device and is never sent to us. Your device’s backup settings may include app data in backups managed by Apple.

Recordings and the microphone

Sound Sprout never uses your microphone. A recording captures only the sounds the app itself makes while you play. Recordings stay on your device unless you choose to share them.

Sharing

If you tap Share, Apple’s share sheet lets you send a recording to a destination you choose. The receiving app or service handles the shared copy under its own policies. Deleting a recording in Sound Sprout does not delete copies you have already shared.

Apple services

The app may ask Apple to show its standard rating prompt. Apple handles ratings and reviews under its own policies, and Sound Sprout does not learn what rating you give. There are no in-app purchases in this version.

Children

Sound Sprout is made for a general audience, including families. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no personal information from children.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual recordings from the Recordings screen. Removing the app deletes all of its data from your device. Device backups and copies you shared may remain under the relevant device or service settings.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send and use it only to answer your request. Please do not send sensitive personal information or a child’s personal information in a support message.

Changes and contact

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date. Questions about privacy? Email admin@dotairstudio.com.
