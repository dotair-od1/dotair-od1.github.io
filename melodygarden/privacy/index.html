Privacy policy

Melody Garden privacy policy

Effective date: [PUBLICATION DATE]. Publisher: DOTAIR LLC (Dotair Studio). Contact: admin@dotairstudio.com.

Melody Garden lets you play musical instruments and save recordings of sounds created inside the app.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your note and rhythm patterns, recordings, favorites, last selected instrument, and limited milestones used to decide when to request an App Store review. These remain in the app’s local storage. We do not operate a server that receives these items. Your device’s backup settings may include app data in backups managed by Apple.

Microphone and accounts

Melody Garden does not access your microphone, ask you to create an account, or require personal information to play. Recording captures sounds generated by the app itself.

Advertising and analytics

This version contains no advertising, third-party analytics SDKs, or tracking technologies. We do not collect usage histories, device identifiers, or location through the app.

Sharing

If you choose Share, Apple’s system share sheet lets you send a recording to a destination you select. The receiving app or service handles the shared copy under its own policies. Deleting the recording from Melody Garden does not delete shared copies.

Apple services

The app may ask Apple to display its native rating prompt. Apple handles ratings and reviews under its own policies. Melody Garden does not learn what rating you give. There are no in-app purchases in this version.

Children

Children and adults can play without providing a name, age, or contact details. A parent or guardian can contact Dotair Studio with questions about privacy.

Deletion

You can delete individual recordings from the Recordings screen. Removing the app deletes its local app data; backups and copies you shared may remain under the relevant device or service settings.

Support

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond to your request. Please do not send sensitive personal information or a child’s personal information in a support message.

Changes

We will update this policy when the app’s data practices change. Contact admin@dotairstudio.com with privacy questions.
