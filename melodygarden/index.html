App support

Melody Garden

A free music playground for iPhone and iPad. Play four instruments, then record and share what you make. Melody Garden is published by Dotair Studio, the studio name of DOTAIR LLC.

Where to get it

  • iPhone & iPad: coming soon to the App Store.

The instruments

  • Rainbow Harp: tap or sweep across eight colorful strings to find your own tune.
  • Melody Grid: plant notes in a grid and hear your melody loop.
  • Rhythm Party: tap the six pieces of a drum kit and hear each one respond. Record a beat at your own pace.
  • String Studio: pluck strings of different lengths and explore warm or bright tones, with optional echo.

Common questions

  • How do I start? Melody Garden opens Rainbow Harp on first launch. Tap or sweep the strings. Tap Explore at the upper left for the other instruments.
  • How do I record? Tap Record and play. The red pause-style icon finishes and saves the take. Use the play and share icons beside Record to listen or share. The app records its own generated sounds, not your microphone.
  • Where are my recordings? Tap the library icon at the upper right. You can play, favorite, share, or delete a recording. Playing a row plays only that take; Play all starts a sequential or shuffled queue.
  • Why can’t I hear anything? Raise the device’s media volume and check the selected audio output, including Bluetooth headphones. If another app is playing sound, pause it to hear Melody Garden more clearly.
  • Can I use landscape? Yes. Turn off the device’s rotation lock and rotate the screen.
  • Does it work offline? The instruments and local recording library work offline. Sharing destinations and Apple services may need internet access.
  • Do recordings sync? Melody Garden does not provide its own cross-device sync. Share recordings to move copies between devices. Keep a shared backup of recordings you want to preserve before deleting the app.
  • What does it cost? This version is free and has no in-app purchases.
  • Which languages are supported? The app interface is currently English. Localized App Store descriptions do not change the interface language.

Get help

Need a hand? Email admin@dotairstudio.com with your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are helpful, but please remove personal information.

Privacy and your data

  • Privacy policy

What happens to my data? Melody Garden has no ads, accounts, third-party analytics or tracking, and it does not use your microphone. Your recordings and patterns stay in the app on your device unless you share them. Read the full privacy policy.
