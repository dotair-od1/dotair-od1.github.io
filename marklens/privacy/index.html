Privacy policy

Marklens privacy policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026. Publisher: DOTAIR LLC (Dotair Studio). Contact: admin@dotairstudio.com.

Marklens is a Mac app for reading Markdown and Mermaid files. We do not collect your data.

What we collect

Nothing. Marklens has no account and no analytics, advertising or tracking code. We run no server for the app, and Marklens never uploads your documents to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your Mac

To work, Marklens stores these on your Mac, inside the app’s private storage:

  • your settings, such as theme, reading width, font size and export choices
  • recently opened files and folders
  • the view and window position you last used for a document
  • permission to reopen folders and a custom CSS file you chose

We cannot see any of this. Deleting the app and its container removes it.

Web content in your documents

Marklens renders documents on your Mac and does not load images or media from the web. A remote image in a document is shown as its alternative text.

If you click a web link in a document, it opens in your default browser, which handles it under its own policies.

Quick Look and thumbnails

The Marklens Quick Look and thumbnail extensions read the file you preview to draw it on your Mac. They have no network access.

Information from Apple

Apple handles App Store downloads under its own privacy policy. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your Mac’s settings, Apple may give us aggregated reports and crash information. These do not include the content of your documents.

Children

Marklens is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Support

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond to your request. Please remove anything private from a file before you send it to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Contact admin@dotairstudio.com with privacy questions.
