App support

Marklens

A Mac app for reading Markdown and Mermaid files, with every diagram drawn in place. Its full name on the App Store is Marklens: Markdown & Diagrams. Marklens is published by Dotair Studio, the studio name of DOTAIR LLC.

Where to get it

  • Mac: coming soon to the Mac App Store. Marklens requires macOS 15 or later.

Common questions

  • Which files can Marklens open? Markdown (.md, .markdown, .mdown, .mkd, .mkdn, .mdx) and Mermaid (.mmd, .mermaid). The folder sidebar lists .md, .markdown, .mmd and .mermaid files.
  • Can I edit files in Marklens? No. Marklens is a reader. Edit in any other app, and Marklens reloads the file when you save.
  • How do I make Marklens open my Markdown files by default? In the Finder, select a Markdown file and choose File > Get Info. Under Open With, choose Marklens, then click Change All.
  • How do I switch views? Use the toolbar, or press Command-1 for Document, Command-2 for Chart, Command-3 for Source and Command-4 for Side by Side. Mermaid files have no Document view.
  • Why is an image missing, or why does Marklens ask for folder access? macOS only lets Marklens read what you choose. If a document uses an image from another location, click the banner to grant access to its folder, or choose File > Grant Folder Access…. You can also open the whole folder with File > Open Folder….
  • Does Marklens need the internet? No. Documents and diagrams render on your Mac, and Marklens never goes online.
  • Why does an image show only its description? Marklens does not load images from the web. An image whose address starts with http is shown as its alternative text. Images stored on your Mac next to the document are shown normally.
  • Why doesn’t audio or video in my document play? Audio and video files stored next to a document do not play in the App Store version.
  • How do I export? For a whole document, choose File > Export Document > HTML… or PDF…. For a diagram, right-click it, or use the toolbar’s Export menu in Chart or Side by Side.
  • Is there a command-line tool? Not in the App Store version, because macOS sandboxing prevents it from reading the paths you give it.
  • Why don’t Quick Look or Finder thumbnails show Marklens previews? Open Marklens once, then open System Settings > General > Login Items & Extensions and make sure the Marklens Quick Look extensions are on. Thumbnails can take a moment to refresh. Quick Look shows each diagram as a labeled card. Open the file in Marklens to see the diagram itself.
  • What does it cost? Marklens is free and has no in-app purchases.
  • Which languages are supported? The interface is in English. Documents in Chinese, Japanese and other languages display normally.

Get help

Need a hand? Email admin@dotairstudio.com with your macOS version, the Marklens version (Marklens > About Marklens) and a short description of what happened. A small file that shows the problem helps, but please remove anything private from it first.

Privacy and your data

  • Privacy policy

What happens to my data? Marklens collects none. It has no account, analytics, ads or tracking, and your documents stay on your Mac. Read the full privacy policy.
