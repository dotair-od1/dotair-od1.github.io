Privacy policy

Practice Time privacy policy

Effective October 23, 2026. Practice Time is made by DOTAIR LLC (“we”). In short: the app works entirely on your device, and we don’t collect your data.

What we collect

Nothing. Practice Time has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it doesn’t send your information to us or to anyone else.

Microphone and recordings

  • With your permission, the app uses the microphone to recognize when you’re playing your instrument, so it can run the practice timer. This sound is analyzed on your device as you play and is not stored or uploaded.
  • If you choose to record a practice session, the recording is kept in temporary storage on your device and replaced by your next recording. It leaves your device only if you share or save it yourself, for example with the iOS share sheet.
  • You can turn off microphone access at any time in Settings → Practice Time.

Data stored on your device

Your practice history and app settings, such as your chosen instrument and sensitivity, are saved only on your device. Your device’s own backups, such as iCloud Backup, may include them under Apple’s terms. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Apple services

Apple may ask you to rate the app using its standard App Store prompt, and Apple handles that rating. The App Store may also share anonymous usage and crash statistics with us if you’ve agreed to share them with app developers in your device settings.

Children

Because the app doesn’t collect personal information, it can be used by musicians of any age.

Changes and contact

If we change how the app handles data, we’ll update this page and its effective date. Questions? Email admin@dotairstudio.com.
