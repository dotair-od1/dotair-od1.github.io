Privacy policy

Practice Time privacy policy

Effective November 20, 2026. Practice Time is made by DOTAIR LLC (“we”). In short: your practice sound, history, recordings and notes stay on your device. We don’t know who you are, and a lesson recording is sent to be written up only if you agree.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you. Practice Time has no accounts, advertising or tracking, and we never receive your name, email, recordings or notes.

The app sends anonymous usage statistics through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. Examples are how long a practice session lasted or whether a goal was set. They are counted with a scrambled identifier that cannot be traced back to you or your device, and they are not combined with data from other apps.

Microphone and recordings

  • With your permission, the app uses the microphone to recognize when you’re playing your instrument, so it can run the practice timer. This sound is analyzed on your device as you play and is not stored or uploaded.
  • If you choose to record a practice session, the recording is saved on your device. It leaves your device only if you share or save it yourself, for example with the iOS share sheet.
  • You can turn off microphone access at any time in Settings → Practice Time.

Lesson notes

  • In the Teaching tab you can record a spoken summary of a lesson. The recording is saved on your device.
  • If you ask for written notes, the app first asks whether the recording may be sent. Only after you agree is that recording sent to Google’s Gemini service, which turns it into the written feedback and homework list. If you don’t agree, nothing is sent and you keep the recording.
  • The recording is sent without your name or any account. We don’t receive or keep a copy, and the written notes are saved only on your device. Google handles the recording under its terms for this service and may keep it for a limited time to prevent abuse.
  • Sound from your practice sessions is never sent.

Data stored on your device

Your practice history, recordings, lesson notes and app settings, such as your chosen instrument and sensitivity, are saved only on your device. Your device’s own backups, such as iCloud Backup, may include them under Apple’s terms. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Apple services

Apple may ask you to rate the app using its standard App Store prompt, and Apple handles that rating. The App Store may also share anonymous usage and crash statistics with us if you’ve agreed to share them with app developers in your device settings.

Children

The app doesn’t collect personal information, so it can be used by musicians of any age. A lesson recording is sent to be written up only after someone agrees in the app; for a child, a parent or teacher should make that choice.

Changes and contact

If we change how the app handles data, we’ll update this page and its effective date. Questions? Email admin@dotairstudio.com.
