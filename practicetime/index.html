App support

Practice Time

A practice timer for musicians that listens for your instrument and counts only the time you actually spend playing. Practice Time is made by Dotair Studio, the studio name of DOTAIR LLC.

Where to get it

  • iPhone: available on the App Store.

Common questions

  • Why does the app need the microphone? It listens for your instrument so it can start and pause the timer automatically. The sound is analyzed on your iPhone and is never uploaded.
  • The timer isn’t picking up my playing. Check that microphone access is turned on in Settings → Practice Time, then try adjusting the sensitivity in the app.
  • Where is my practice history kept? Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app also deletes your history.

Get help

Email admin@dotairstudio.com with questions, problems or feedback. To help us sort it out quickly, please tell us:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version, and the app version if you know it;
  • what you were doing and what happened;
  • a screenshot, if you have one.

Privacy and your data

  • Privacy policy

Practice Time keeps your data on your device. For any data question, email admin@dotairstudio.com.
