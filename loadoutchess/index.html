Get help

Email admin@dotairstudio.com with questions, problems or feedback. To help us sort it out quickly, please tell us:

  • whether you play on the web, iPhone or iPad, and which version of the app if you know it;
  • what you were doing and what happened;
  • a screenshot, if you have one.

Please don’t send passwords or PINs by email.
